KICKOFF Competition Standard

Fair Play Rules

These rules define the behaviour expected from every KICKOFF player so tournaments remain competitive, honest, respectful, and enjoyable.

1. Play to Win, Not to Cheat

Every match must be played fairly. Players must use the supported game and platform as specified by the tournament and must not use cheats, hacks, exploits, modified clients, or unauthorized assistance.

2. Honest Results

Submit the actual result of the match. Never fabricate, edit, reuse, or manipulate screenshots or other evidence. Do not pressure an opponent to accept an incorrect result.

3. Match Coordination

Players should make a genuine effort to contact their opponents and agree on a suitable time. When WhatsApp coordination is enabled, use the contact information only for legitimate match coordination and do not spam or harass another player.

4. Deadlines and Inactivity

Respect scheduled match times, check-in requirements, grace periods, and play deadlines. Repeated inactivity or failure to participate may result in strikes, walkovers, removal, or other administrative action.

5. Respect Your Opponent

  • No insults, threats, intimidation, discrimination, or harassment.
  • Do not deliberately disconnect, delay, or sabotage a match to gain an unfair advantage.
  • Do not share another player's private contact information outside legitimate tournament coordination.

6. Disputes

If players disagree about a result or conduct, provide truthful evidence and allow administrators to review the case. Do not attempt to retaliate against an opponent or manipulate the dispute process.

7. Match Fixing and Collusion

Players must not intentionally lose, trade wins, share accounts, arrange fake matches, or collude to manipulate brackets, standings, rankings, prizes, or payouts.

8. Reporting Violations

Report suspected cheating, false results, harassment, match fixing, or other serious violations through the available reporting or dispute tools. Reports should be factual and made in good faith.

9. Penalties

Depending on severity and evidence, violations may lead to warnings, strikes, result corrections, walkovers, tournament removal, prize or payout cancellation, temporary suspension, or permanent bans.

10. Fair Play Standard

The goal is simple: compete hard, respect your opponent, report results honestly, and leave every tournament confident that the outcome was earned fairly.
